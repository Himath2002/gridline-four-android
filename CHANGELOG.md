## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-21

### Added

- Configurable 5 × 6, 6 × 7, and 7 × 8 four-in-a-row boards.
- Local-player and tactical-computer modes.
- In-memory profiles with six original vector avatars.
- Coral/gold and jade/violet disc palettes.
- Session statistics, restart, local-play undo, and explicit navigation.
- Pure Java rules engine with typed move outcomes and defensive snapshots.
- Focused JVM tests for board rules, undo, and computer decisions.
- Material 3 interface, View Binding, lifecycle-aware state, and privacy-first backup rules.
- Android CI, publication documentation, and original repository visuals.
